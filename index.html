Why these are different

Genuinely private

There is no account, no server and no analytics on your inputs. Your dates are stored only in your own browser, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Shows the reasoning

Every tool explains how the number was reached and where the estimate weakens — irregular cycles, scan dating, the limits of averages.

Readable by everyone

Colour is never the only signal. Every chart carries labels, a legend and a data table, and the palette is verified for colour-vision deficiency.

These tools are not contraception and not medical advice. They estimate from cycle averages and standard formulas. Ovulation timing shifts, scan dating beats calendar dating, and every pregnancy differs. Always consult a qualified doctor about your own situation.
